FlowMD Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FlowMD is a personal study planner for NEET-PG preparation. This page explains what data the app stores, why, how long it is kept, and how you can export or delete it.

1. What data is stored

FlowMD is offline-first: your study progress is stored only on your device in the browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and no account is required.

  • Completed videos, daily study history, streaks, plans and targets, and app preferences (theme, study source).
  • All of it stays in your browser's local storage on this device.

2. Why it is stored

The data exists only to run the app locally and show you accurate analytics and streaks. It is not sold, shared, uploaded, or used for advertising. FlowMD makes no analytics or tracking network calls.

3. How long it is kept

Your data persists on the device until you clear your browser data, uninstall the app, or reset app data from Profile. Because storage is device-local, clearing browser data or uninstalling erases your progress — export a backup first.

4. Export your data

You can download a JSON backup of everything at any time: open the app, go to Profile → Backup → Export Backup. The file is yours to keep, and it stays importable across future app versions. Move between devices by importing it on the new one.

5. Delete your data

You can erase all data at any time: open the app, go to Profile → Data Management → Reset All App Data, or clear this site's browser data. This action cannot be undone.

6. Cloud sync (roadmap)

Cloud sync is on the roadmap as an optional feature. If and when it ships, this page will be updated to describe what it stores; until then, no data leaves your device.
